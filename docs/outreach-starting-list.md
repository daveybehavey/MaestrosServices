# Outreach Starting List

If you do not know where to begin, start here in this order.

The goal is to get a few real local mentions fast, then keep building from there.

## First 10 Places To Reach Out

### 1. Victoria Chamber of Commerce

- Website: https://www.victoriachamber.org/
- Why start here: strong local business authority and a good fit for a regional service business.
- Best ask: member listing, directory listing, or business feature.

### 2. WestShore Chamber of Commerce

- Website: https://westshorechamber.org/
- Why start here: relevant to the Greater Victoria side of the service area.
- Best ask: member listing, sponsor credit, or event mention.

### 3. Saanich Peninsula Chamber of Commerce

- Website: https://peninsulachamber.ca/
- Why start here: useful for Sidney, North Saanich, Central Saanich, and nearby areas.
- Best ask: member directory listing or member spotlight.

### 4. Duncan Cowichan Chamber of Commerce

- Website: https://www.duncancc.bc.ca/
- Why start here: strong authority for the Cowichan side of the service area.
- Best ask: member listing or community/business feature.

### 5. A Landscape Supplier You Actually Use

- Ask the supplier where you buy mulch, gravel, rock, or materials.
- Why start here: they already know your work and are more likely to say yes.
- Best ask: preferred contractor mention or supplier referral page.

### 6. A Fence Material Supplier

- Ask any fence supply shop or lumber yard you buy from.
- Why start here: fence work is one of the clear service lines on the site.
- Best ask: preferred installer mention or partner referral.

### 7. A Property Manager

- Ask a local property manager who handles rental or strata upkeep.
- Why start here: they often need reliable cleanup and maintenance work.
- Best ask: vendor listing or contractor referral contact.

### 8. A Local Realtor

- Ask a realtor who regularly works with homes that need pre-sale cleanup.
- Why start here: curb appeal services like power washing, fencing, and cleanup are easy to refer.
- Best ask: testimonial swap or preferred contractor mention.

### 9. A Happy Customer With A Business

- Ask a past customer if they have a business page, community group, or local network.
- Why start here: personal relationships convert better than cold outreach.
- Best ask: testimonial, referral, or partner page mention.

### 10. A Community Organization Or Neighborhood Group

- Ask about local association pages, community boards, or volunteer groups.
- Why start here: good for local proof and trust, especially if you sponsor or help on a project.
- Best ask: sponsor credit or community mention.

## Best Starting Path By Location

### If You Want Greater Victoria Coverage First

- Victoria Chamber of Commerce
- WestShore Chamber of Commerce
- Saanich Peninsula Chamber of Commerce
- local suppliers
- property managers and realtors in Victoria, Langford, Colwood, Saanich, and Sidney

### If You Want Cowichan Coverage First

- Duncan Cowichan Chamber of Commerce
- local suppliers in Duncan / Cowichan
- property managers and realtors in Shawnigan Lake, Mill Bay, Cobble Hill, Cowichan Bay, Maple Bay, and Duncan

## What To Send Them

Keep it simple:

- business name
- website
- phone number
- service area summary
- one sentence about what you do
- 2 to 3 project photos
- a short testimonial if you have one

## Best First Ask

Use this wording:

"Would you be open to a member listing, sponsor credit, or partner mention for a local residential landscaping business?"

That is clearer and easier to say yes to than asking for an SEO backlink.
