# Local Services Ads (LSA) Onboarding Checklist

Start this now while organic cadence runs.

## Goal

Get LSA approved and ready to launch with a controlled budget cap.

## Setup Checklist

- [ ] Confirm service categories to include first:
  - [ ] Landscaping
  - [ ] Power washing
  - [ ] Fence work (if eligible)
  - [ ] Rock work / walls (if eligible)
- [ ] Confirm initial service areas (start tight, then expand).
- [ ] Create/verify Google account owner access.
- [ ] Start LSA signup.
- [ ] Submit required business verification details.
- [ ] Complete background/license/insurance steps if requested.
- [ ] Connect and optimize Google Business Profile details.
- [ ] Add services and business description.
- [ ] Add opening hours and lead response process.
- [ ] Set weekly lead budget cap.
- [ ] Enable lead notifications on both phones.

## Launch Rules

- Start with a conservative weekly lead cap.
- Keep service area focused in launch phase.
- Respond to new leads within 1 hour during work hours.
- Pause quickly if lead quality is low for 2 straight weeks.

## Weekly Review (After Launch)

- [ ] Leads received
- [ ] Qualified leads
- [ ] Booked jobs
- [ ] Cost per qualified lead
- [ ] Cost per booked job
- [ ] Keep / tighten / pause decision
