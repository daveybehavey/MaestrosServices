# Local Proof And Backlink Outreach List

The goal is not random backlink building.

The goal is to get real local mentions from people and organizations that already have community trust.

## Priority Targets

### Chambers and Business Groups

These are the most obvious local trust targets because they are community-facing and business-focused.

- [Victoria Chamber of Commerce](https://www.victoriachamber.org/)
- [WestShore Chamber of Commerce](https://www.westshore.bc.ca/)
- [Saanich Peninsula Chamber of Commerce](https://peninsulachamber.ca/)
- [Sooke Region Chamber of Commerce](https://www.sookeregionchamber.com/)
- [Duncan Chamber of Commerce](https://duncanchamber.com/)

Best ask:

- member directory listing
- sponsor credit
- event mention
- member spotlight
- local business feature

### Community and Local Guide Sites

These are useful when they publish real local business info or project stories.

- [Cowichan Bay BIA](https://cowichanbay.com/)
- local community association pages
- neighborhood newsletters
- local "shop local" guides
- local visitor or business directory pages

Best ask:

- business listing
- project feature
- sponsor page credit
- community partnership mention

### Supplier and Partner Pages

These are often easier to get than chamber links and can be very valuable if the relationship is real.

Target types:

- landscape supply yards
- mulch and gravel suppliers
- fence material suppliers
- rock and hardscape suppliers
- pressure washing supply partners
- property managers
- realtors
- home inspectors
- local builders

Best ask:

- "preferred contractor" mention
- supplier referral page
- testimonial with a link
- project showcase mention

## Outreach Priorities By Region

### Greater Victoria

Focus on:

- Victoria Chamber

### Cowichan Valley

Focus on:

- Duncan Chamber

- Sooke region community listings if the work reaches that far
- Cowichan-area suppliers and partner pages

## What To Offer

When you reach out, make the ask easy to say yes to.

Good offers:

- a short business profile
- a project photo set
- a before-and-after story
- a testimonial swap
- a sponsor credit
- a local partner mention

## Message Template

Subject: Local partner listing for Maestros Services

Hi [Name],

We are a residential landscaping company serving lower-mid Vancouver Island, and we are looking to build a few local proof links and partner mentions from trusted community businesses.

If you offer a member directory, sponsor page, supplier listing, or local business feature, we would be happy to provide:

- a short business description
- logo
- service summary
- project photos
- testimonial copy

If you think a listing or mention makes sense, I can send everything in one reply.

Thanks,
[Name]

## Proof Assets To Prepare First

Before outreach, have these ready:

- short business description
- logo files
- phone number
- website URL
- service area summary
- 3 to 5 strong project photos
- 1 to 3 short testimonials
- one sentence about the main services

## What Counts As A Good Win

Good outcomes include:

- a chamber directory listing
- a real supplier or partner mention
- a community sponsor credit
- a featured project page with a link back
- a testimonial on another local site

Do not waste time on:

- spam directories
- paid link farms
- fake guest post networks
- generic SEO package sellers
