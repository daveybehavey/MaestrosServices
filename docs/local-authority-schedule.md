# Local Authority Schedule

This is the weekly routine for building local backlinks, mentions, and trust.

The goal is to get real proof from real local relationships, not random SEO links.

## Weekly Rhythm

### Monday

- Review the outreach list.
- Pick 3 primary targets and 2 backup targets.
- Check which service or project is easiest to pitch this week.
- Pull together a short description, logo, website link, and 2 to 3 photos.

### Tuesday

- Send 2 outreach messages.
- Prioritize one chamber/business group and one supplier or partner.
- Ask for a directory listing, sponsor mention, or partner page mention.

### Wednesday

- Follow up on last week’s messages.
- Send one testimonial or project story to any partner who asked for more details.
- Publish or share one useful blog post that a partner could reasonably reference.

### Thursday

- Ask one happy customer for a review.
- Ask one customer or partner if they know a local group, supplier, or community page that accepts listings.
- Log any replies and next steps.

### Friday

- Update the scorecard.
- Record:
  - messages sent
  - replies
  - listings live
  - mentions gained
  - reviews requested
- Decide what to repeat next week.

## Four-Week Rotation

### Week 1: Chambers

- Send to chamber and business association targets first.
- Goal: 1 directory or membership listing.

### Week 2: Suppliers

- Contact gravel, mulch, fence, and hardscape suppliers.
- Goal: 1 partner mention or preferred contractor page.

### Week 3: Community

- Contact local community pages, BIAs, and neighborhood groups.
- Goal: 1 sponsor credit or community mention.

### Week 4: Property-Related Partners

- Contact realtors, property managers, home inspectors, and builders.
- Goal: 1 referral relationship or project showcase mention.

## Simple Outreach Volume

Start small and stay consistent:

- 4 to 6 outreach messages per week
- 1 follow-up block per week
- 1 review request per week at minimum
- 1 proof asset update per week

## What To Send Each Time

Keep the packet simple:

- business name
- website
- phone number
- service area summary
- 1 sentence on what you do
- 2 to 3 photos
- short testimonial if available

## Success Targets

Good early wins include:

- one chamber listing
- one supplier mention
- one community listing
- one testimonial on another site
- one partner referral conversation

The main rule is to keep the outreach steady and real. Local authority grows from repeated, credible contact.
