# Lead Growth Playbook

This is a practical SEO and lead-generation plan for Maestros Services.

The goal is not to "post keywords" and hope. The goal is to make the site:

- easier for Google to understand
- more useful for real customers
- faster at turning visits into calls and quote requests
- stronger in local trust signals

## Priority Order

1. Make the homepage, service pages, area pages, and quote page stronger.
2. Publish one useful GBP update per week.
3. Ask for reviews consistently.
4. Add local proof links and mentions from real partners.
5. Update existing pages based on Search Console data.

## What To Improve On Existing Pages

### Homepage

Keep the homepage focused on the clearest reasons to hire the business.

What it should do well:

- Say what the business does in the first screen.
- Say where it works in plain language.
- Make the phone number and quote form obvious.
- Show trust proof near the top.
- Link to the main money pages fast.

Suggested improvements:

- Keep the hero focused on a small set of high-value services.
- Mention the main service areas naturally, not as a long keyword list.
- Add one short section for "what to send for a fast quote."
- Keep the review/testimonial proof visible near the top.

### Services Index

This page should act like a clean service hub.

What it should do well:

- Group services by search intent and common customer needs.
- Let visitors click through to the exact service they want.
- Keep copy specific and local without sounding stuffed.

Suggested improvements:

- Add a short intro that says these are residential landscaping services on Vancouver Island.
- Keep the service cards readable and benefit-focused.
- Add a short "browse by area" link near the top and bottom.

### Individual Service Pages

These are some of the most important pages on the site.

Each service page should answer:

- what the service is
- who needs it
- what is included
- when to schedule it
- what makes the result better
- what questions people ask before buying

Suggested structure for each service page:

1. Clear title with the service name.
2. Short intro that includes the main phrase naturally.
3. "What is included" section.
4. "When to schedule" section.
5. Before/after or outcome examples.
6. FAQs.
7. Related service links.
8. Quote CTA.

Search-style phrasing to use naturally:

- lawn mowing in Victoria
- hedge trimming in Saanich
- mulch installation in Duncan
- power washing for driveways
- driveway grading on Vancouver Island

Do not repeat the same phrase five times. Use the phrase once or twice, then write normally.

### Area Pages

Area pages are good for local relevance if they are written like real local pages.

What they should do well:

- name the city or area clearly
- mention a few neighborhoods or nearby places
- connect local conditions to the service
- link to relevant services and nearby areas

Suggested improvements:

- Make sure each area page has a unique opening paragraph.
- Include one paragraph that explains why the service matters in that area.
- Mention nearby neighborhoods and landmarks only when it feels natural.

### Service + Area Pages

These are strong pages because they match exact search intent.

Examples of intent:

- lawn mowing Victoria BC
- hedge trimming Duncan
- mulch delivery Shawnigan Lake
- fence repair Langford

What these pages should do:

- keep the title clean and specific
- explain the service in that location
- include a short local proof block
- add one or two real FAQs
- link back to the service page and the area page

### Projects Page

This page should prove the work is real and visible.

Good project page content:

- location
- service performed
- problem before the job
- what was done
- result after the job
- photo gallery if possible

Suggested project title style:

- Fence refresh in Langford
- Gravel driveway reset in Shawnigan Lake
- Mulch and bed cleanup in Saanich
- Power wash and edge cleanup in Victoria

### Quote Page

This page should be the fastest path to a lead.

What it should do well:

- reduce friction
- explain what info to send
- make calling easier than searching around
- reassure the visitor that replies are quick

Suggested improvements:

- Keep the form short.
- Keep the call-to-action visible above and below the form.
- Repeat the "reply within 1 hour during work hours" message.
- Add a short checklist for what to include in the message.

### Blog

The blog should be useful, not random.

Good blog topics are usually:

- seasonal
- problem-solving
- local
- service-adjacent

Good topic styles:

- how often should you mow a lawn in Vancouver Island weather
- when should you trim hedges in BC
- how to stop weeds from coming back in mulch beds
- spring cleanup checklist for homeowners
- fall yard prep before the rain starts
- how to know if your driveway needs grading

## Content That Matches Real Search

Use content that sounds like a homeowner searching for help.

Good keyword themes:

- residential landscaping Victoria
- lawn care Saanich
- hedge trimming Duncan
- mulch installation Shawnigan Lake
- driveway grading Vancouver Island
- power washing patios and driveways
- seasonal yard cleanup

Good content rules:

- Write for the person, not the algorithm.
- Use the main phrase once in the title and once in the intro when it fits.
- Add useful details like timing, scope, and common issues.
- Avoid awkward keyword blocks.
- Avoid trying to sound "SEO optimized" in a visible way.

## Weekly GBP Plan

There is no magic posting time that makes SEO work.

The real win is consistency and usefulness.

Use a simple weekly rhythm:

- Post once per week.
- Use a normal mid-morning weekday if that is easiest to maintain.
- Keep posts short and clear.
- Include one call to action.

Weekly post ideas:

- this week's service availability
- seasonal reminder
- before/after project
- offer or limited-time deal
- quick tip tied to a service
- short photo update from a job

Suggested GBP post formula:

1. What service you are highlighting.
2. Why it matters now.
3. A photo if possible.
4. A short CTA to call or request a quote.

Example post topics:

- Spring mulch refresh now booking
- Fence repair and small fixes available this week
- Power washing to clean up patios and driveways
- Seasonal cleanup spots opening up

## Local Proof and Backlinks

The best links are local and believable.

Good sources of local proof:

- supplier websites
- local chambers or business groups
- neighborhood or community organizations
- sponsor pages
- realtor or property manager partner pages
- local blogs or news features
- testimonials from local vendors or customers

What to ask for:

- a business listing
- a partner mention
- a simple sponsor credit
- a testimonial on a partner page
- a project mention with a link back

Avoid:

- spam directories
- low-quality backlink packages
- generic article farms
- fake "local SEO" networks

## Conversion Fast Checklist

Traffic only matters if it turns into leads.

What helps conversions:

- tap-to-call on mobile
- quote form that is short and easy
- clear service area language
- trust proof near the CTA
- review count and testimonials
- before/after photos
- fast response promise
- visible phone number in the header

Best conversion moves:

- Put a call or text button in the header.
- Repeat CTA blocks after key sections.
- Keep the form short.
- Put "what to send for a fast quote" near the form.
- Show one or two short trust statements near the quote CTA.
- Make sure the mobile experience is very fast.

## 30-Day Action Plan

Week 1:

- Tighten homepage and quote page messaging.
- Add or improve 3 service pages.
- Make sure all CTA buttons are obvious.

Week 2:

- Publish one useful blog post.
- Post on GBP once.
- Ask for 1 to 3 reviews.

Week 3:

- Improve 3 area pages.
- Add one local proof mention or backlink.
- Review Search Console queries for page ideas.

Week 4:

- Update one project page.
- Publish another GBP update.
- Refresh one page based on what Search Console shows.

## What Success Looks Like

You are moving in the right direction if:

- Search Console shows more impressions for service and area queries.
- The quote page gets more clicks.
- GBP gets more calls and website visits.
- Reviews are increasing steadily.
- More leads mention a service or area that matches the site.
