## Targets

- Week 2 target: +30% total inquiries vs baseline.
- Week 4 target: +50% total inquiries vs baseline, or minimum 8 qualified inquiries/week.

## Zero-Baseline Rule

If baseline total inquiries = 0, use absolute targets instead of percentage growth:

- Week 1: 3+ total inquiries
- Week 2: 5+ total inquiries and 3+ qualified inquiries
- Week 4: 8+ qualified inquiries

## Decision Rules

- Keep channels producing booked jobs.
- Drop channels with low-quality inquiries for 2 straight weeks.
- If a channel produces inquiries but zero qualified leads for 2 weeks, pause it.
