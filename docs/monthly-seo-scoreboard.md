# Monthly SEO Scoreboard

Use this once per month to see whether the site is actually producing more traffic and leads.

## What To Track

### Search Console

- Total clicks
- Total impressions
- Average position for top service terms
- Average position for top area terms
- Top pages by clicks
- Top queries by clicks

### Google Business Profile

- Website clicks
- Calls
- Direction requests
- Messages
- Post views
- Review count

### Lead Metrics

- Quote form submissions
- Phone calls from the website
- Text messages from the website
- Calls from GBP
- Quote-to-job conversion rate

### Content Output

- Blog posts published
- GBP posts published
- Pages updated
- Outreach emails sent
- Outreach replies received

## Monthly Review Questions

1. Which pages got the most clicks?
2. Which pages produced the most quote requests?
3. Which service terms moved up?
4. Which area pages need better copy or proof?
5. Which blog posts are sending visitors to money pages?
6. Which outreach efforts created real mentions or links?

## Good Targets

- Publish at least 4 GBP posts per month
- Publish 2 to 4 useful blog posts per month
- Update 2 to 4 existing pages per month
- Send 8 to 16 outreach messages per month
- Earn at least 1 to 3 new local mentions or links per month

## What Success Looks Like

- More impressions on service and area pages
- More clicks into the quote page
- More calls from GBP and the website
- More review volume
- More local mentions from suppliers, partners, and community pages

## What To Do If Results Stall

- Tighten the page title and intro copy
- Add more internal links from blog posts to money pages
- Improve photos and proof blocks
- Ask for more reviews
- Send outreach to a different set of local partners
- Check Search Console queries and write content that matches what people are already searching
