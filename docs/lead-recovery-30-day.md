# 30-Day Lead Recovery Playbook (Low Budget, Local Tone)

Use this as the operating plan for the next 4 weeks.

## Main Goal

Increase qualified calls, texts, and quote requests using mostly organic channels.

## Fast-Turn Offers to Push Everywhere

- Fence Refresh
- Rock Work Refresh
- Power Wash Reset
- Mulch Refresh
- Add-on menu: gutter cleaning (ground-accessible), light pruning, moss/algae treatment

Use these priority offers in:
- Google Business Profile posts
- Facebook page and local groups
- Homepage and quote page messaging
- Call/text conversations

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not publish client photos unless you have clear permission.
- Use non-identifiable or approved images only.
- Respond to new leads within 1 hour during work hours.
- Keep weekly capacity visible so you can post honest "available this week" updates.

## Weekly Cadence

### Google Business Profile (3 posts per week)

- 2 service/result posts (process, outcome, neighborhood)
- 1 availability post (open spots this week)
- Include tracked quote link in every post

### Facebook (5 posts per week)

- 3 posts in local groups/pages with service-area rotation
- 1 Monday "open slot this week" post (non-negotiable)
- 1 trust/proof post (review text, process photo, service checklist)

### Reviews and Referrals

- Ask every completed-job customer for a review within 24 hours.
- Ask them to mention service + location.
- Ask for one neighborhood introduction from happy customers.

## Week-by-Week Focus

### Week 1

- Push fast-turn offers only.
- Publish posting cadence.
- Enforce 1-hour response standard.
- Start baseline tracking (leads + booked jobs).
- Start Local Services Ads onboarding process.

### Week 2

- Keep offers and cadence consistent.
- Target: +30% inquiries vs baseline.
- Keep channels producing qualified bookings.
- Track lead quality by channel, not just lead volume.

### Week 3

- Double down on best-performing channel and offer.
- Reduce low-quality channels.
- Keep Monday open-slot post and review requests running.

### Week 4

- Target: +50% inquiries vs baseline OR at least 8 qualified inquiries/week.
- Keep only channels with booked-job output.
- Plan next 30 days from winning channels and offers.

## Channel Rule

- Keep channels producing booked jobs.
- Drop channels with low-quality inquiries for 2 straight weeks.
- Pause any channel with 0 qualified leads for 2 straight weeks.
