# Low-Touch Operating Plan

This plan is for when you want the business to keep improving without daily attention.

## Principle

Do not try to manage the site every day.
Keep the system simple, batch the work, and let the repo hold the strategy.

## What The Robot Can Handle

- Rewrite and improve site copy when asked.
- Draft GBP post batches.
- Draft blog post batches.
- Update service and area pages in bulk.
- Add internal links between blog posts and money pages.
- Expand the scoreboard or outreach templates.
- Run validation after edits.

## What The Human Should Handle

- Approve the content before it goes live.
- Post to Google Business Profile when a draft is ready.
- Ask for reviews after completed jobs.
- Share photos or project details when available.
- Reply to leads quickly.

## Recommended Cadence

### Monthly

- Review the scoreboard.
- Pick the next 2 to 4 pages to improve.
- Draft 1 to 4 blog posts.
- Draft 4 GBP posts.
- Send 8 to 16 outreach messages.

### After Each Job

- Ask for a Google review.
- Save one good photo for later use.
- Note the service, area, and result for future project proof.

### When You Have Time

- Send a photo or short note and let the repo update the content around it.
- Ask for a batch of GBP posts instead of doing them one by one.

## Best Rule For A Busy Owner

If it is not improving leads, trust, or proof, do not spend time on it this week.

## If You Want The Lightest Possible Workflow

1. Let the repo keep the strategy docs current.
2. Let the robot draft content and page improvements.
3. Only approve and post when the batch is ready.
4. Use monthly review, not daily maintenance.
