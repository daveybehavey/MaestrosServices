On this November job, we turned a bare garden track into a stepping-stone path between the house and shed. The photo above shows the finished route. Here is the work along the way.

## Before: a bare garden track

<img src="/work/path-before-01.jpg" alt="Bare garden track with pallets of pavers beside the shed" width="900" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

The route started as bare ground, with the pavers stacked nearby. This photo shows the space before the stones were laid.

## Preparation: opening up the route

<img src="/work/path-during-dug.jpg" alt="Dug-out garden track with a wheelbarrow and the house behind it" width="900" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

We dug out the route and prepared it for the pavers. The photos show the track during preparation, with the wheelbarrow and compactor on site.

<img src="/work/path-during-compactor.jpg" alt="Compactor on the dug-out path beside a wheelbarrow of soil" width="675" height="900" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

## Laying: one paver at a time

<img src="/work/path-during-kneeling.jpg" alt="Josh kneeling to position a paver along the prepared path" width="900" height="675" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

Josh placed and adjusted the pavers by hand along the prepared route. The finished path follows the garden track across the yard to the house.
