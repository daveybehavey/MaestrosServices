If you are looking into power washing in Victoria, BC, the biggest question is usually not whether cleaning will help.

It is **where to start first**.

Most homeowners have more than one surface that could use attention:

- driveway
- front entry
- walkway
- patio
- deck
- fence

Trying to do everything at once is not always necessary. Starting with the right surface usually gets the biggest visual payoff fastest.

## Start With The Surface That Changes The First Impression

For many homes in Victoria and nearby Saanich, that is usually one of these:

- the driveway
- the front walkway
- the entry landing

Those are the areas people see first, and they are often where moss, algae, dirt, and seasonal buildup show up the fastest.

If one of those surfaces looks dark or slippery, it can make the whole property feel less maintained.

## Driveways Usually Give The Biggest Visual Reset

Driveways are often the strongest first choice when:

- the concrete or pavers have gone dark
- the front of the property looks tired
- there are slippery patches near the edges
- you are getting ready for guests, photos, or a seasonal cleanup

Driveway washing can make the whole approach feel cleaner very quickly.

## Walkways And Entries Matter For Safety Too

Walkways and front entries are not just cosmetic.

They are also common trouble spots for:

- algae
- moss
- damp shade buildup
- slippery foot traffic areas

If you want the safest first improvement, these surfaces may matter more than the biggest square footage.

## Patios And Decks Make Sense Before Summer Use

Patios and deck cleaning often move up the list when:

- you want outdoor space ready for summer
- the surface feels grimy or discolored
- pollen, leaf stain, or damp buildup has settled in
- you want the backyard to match a cleaner front entry

That is also where the term "deck cleaning" often overlaps with power washing searches.

## Fences And Side Surfaces Are Usually Secondary

Fence cleaning, side-yard paths, and less visible surfaces still matter, but they are usually the second phase after the front approach or main gathering areas.

They make the most sense when:

- the front-facing surfaces are already in decent shape
- you are bundling a wider curb-appeal reset
- you want the property to feel consistently clean from one area to another

## Victoria And Saanich Homes Often Deal With The Same Pattern

In Victoria, Saanich, and nearby areas, the usual pattern is:

- winter moisture buildup
- shaded sections staying damp longer
- algae or moss returning around edges
- surfaces looking older because of organic staining more than actual damage

That is why the smartest first clean is usually the one that solves both:

- **appearance**
- **slippery buildup**

## A Simple Order If You Are Not Sure

If you are deciding what to clean first, this is a practical order:

1. driveway or front walkway
2. entry landing or front steps
3. patio or deck
4. fence or secondary surfaces

That order usually gives the strongest visible improvement for the least guesswork.
