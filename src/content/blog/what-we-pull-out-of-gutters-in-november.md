When did you last check your gutters? Most people never look until one overflows. This is what we found on a recent job in November — and if yours have not been looked at this year, this is probably what is in there too.

## Clogged, with water going nowhere

![Clogged gutter close-up with black sludge and standing water](/work/gutter-clogged-closeup.jpg)

Black sludge and standing water. A gutter this full cannot drain, so the water sits there — and then it goes over the edge, down the fascia, and onto the ground by the house.

## Where it all comes from

![Roof valley full of pine needles and moss](/work/roof-valley-needles.jpg)

The roof valley was full of pine needles and moss. That is where most of it starts: leaves and needles collect on the roof, wash down in the rain, and settle in the gutter.

![Long gutter run above the deck with a mossy roof behind it](/work/gutter-long-deck.jpg)

Moss holds water against the roof, lifts shingles, and fills the gutters. Moss on the roof and a full gutter underneath go together — we see them on the same jobs, every time.
